The claim

Most engineered biological systems are switches. ReLeaf is a dial.

We used the CcaSR green/red optogenetic system to make Bacillus subtilis express ACC deaminase in proportion to green light intensity, and we drive that light from a microcontroller reading soil salinity and temperature — so the amount of protein manufactured tracks the severity of the stress, continuously. The 0.2 µm hollow-fibre membrane retains the bacteria inside the device; only the permeate reaches the root zone.

Biocontainment: the 0.2 µm membrane is a cell-retention barrier — B. subtilis at 1–2 µm never leaves the device. It is not protein-selective: ACC deaminase (≈36 kDa) passes, and so do small vesicles and debris. A second containment layer is open — see Safety.

Proof it works, layered

Five rungs. Two are standing.

Each rung has to be true for the rung above it to mean anything. Status as of 2026‑08‑13, not as we hope it will be.

1
Light controls expression partial

CcaS, CcaR and the PcpcG2 output are all sequence-verified. Reporter fluorescence vs. calibrated light intensity is the 8/25 milestone — built, not yet characterised. → Measurement

2
Light controls ACC deaminase partial

Construct I — light-gated ACCD — positive since 2026‑07‑28. The α-ketobutyrate activity assay against a constitutive Pveg control has not been run. → Results

3
ACCD crosses the membrane, cells do not not achieved

Blocked upstream. B. subtilis showed no net growth inside the fibre lumen on 2026‑06‑10: lumen OD600 flat and noisy at 0.10–0.19 against a flask control of 0.172 → 0.377. Root cause not established. → Results

4
The permeate does something to a plant not achieved

Root elongation / fresh weight under NaCl, permeate vs. mock. Depends on rung 3. → Results, for what we learned instead

5
Closed loop, end to end partial

The sensing half is closed. Stress → light has run since 2026‑06‑20 (SHT31 → PWM green); the in-line photometer then logged for three weeks and detected the cross-flow pump dying — our own instrument catching a hardware failure. The biology half is open. → Hardware

What we killed, and why

Four payloads entered, one is alive. The kills are on the homepage because the reasoning is the engineering.

ACC deaminase

advanced

Light-gated construct sequence-verified 07‑28. ≈36 kDa, passes the 0.2 µm membrane. Lowers ethylene stress signalling in the root zone. This is the payload.

LEA14

killed

Constructs G and H failed in both the light-driven and the constitutive version. Because the constitutive control failed too, the fault is the payload/junction — not the promoter. That distinction is why we stopped rather than re-tuning.

BoPep4

deprioritised

All seven constructs carry a C-terminal 6×His on Asn23, no protease site. In 5GR8, Asn23's free carboxylate salt-bridges PEPR1 Arg487 at 2.40 / 2.80 Å — the tag removes that contact. Valid secretion reporters, not bioactivity constructs.

GanA-Gala

killed

Constructs L and M failed in both versions, same signature as LEA14. Retired at the same decision point.

What is not true yet

As of 2026‑08‑13. This block is maintained, not decorative.

  • No transfer function measured in-house. Every Hill parameter on this page is Castillo‑Hair 2019, different chassis, different wavelength.
  • Cells do not grow inside the fibre lumen. Candidate causes — O₂ limitation, no fed-batch feed, shear, temperature — and no root cause yet.
  • Second containment layer is open. Membrane retention is net one. Net two was called necessary on 2026‑07‑16 and has no design.
  • Permeate pump not selected; root-zone delivery deferred to a reservoir hand-off.
  • The BoPep4 constructs cannot answer a bioactivity question, and we will not present them as though they can.

Four more open items — photometer linearity and limit of detection, LPA photon flux per duty cycle, the lux→PPFD conversion factor, and per-device bill of materials — are tracked on Results.
