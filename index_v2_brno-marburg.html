The claim

Most engineered cells are switches.
Ours is a dial.

A microcontroller reads soil salinity and temperature, and drives a 520 nm green LED at an intensity proportional to what it read. Inside the chamber, B. subtilis carrying the CcaSR optogenetic system makes ACC deaminase in proportion to that light. The 0.2 µm membrane keeps the bacteria in; only the permeate reaches the roots.

Turn the stress up and the dose rises smoothly. Turn it down and the dose tapers. Nothing in the loop is a threshold.

The machine

We built three instruments to make one device honest

A perfusion bioreactor around a 0.2 µm hollow-fibre module. An in-line photometer that reads cell density without pulling a sample. A dual-wavelength LED array that can hold a set intensity rather than blink. None of them were bought.

Real part numbers, because anyone can write “peristaltic pump”: Biophsep™ mPES HF-E-MI-M020-10-60-P, 0.2 µm, 150 cm², 8 fibres, 1.0 mm lumen · Arduino Uno R4 with a 14-bit ADC · IRLZ44N logic-level MOSFET · BP8G-AGA 0–1 MPa pressure transducer.

The part that worked

Our photometer noticed the pump die

Stress → light has been running since 20 June. Transmembrane pressure has been instrumented since 12 July. Then the in-line photometer logged for three straight weeks and caught a sharp terminal drop that turned out to be the cross-flow pump failing — an instrument we built detecting a fault in the machine it was watching.

The sensing half of the loop is closed. The biology half is not, and we say so.

What would have to be true

One device, then one row

Scaling means replicating the 8-fibre module at constant wall shear and constant flux — the same linear scale-up rule that carried a published process 400-fold without a pilot stage. We keep the physics at the membrane identical and let only the area grow.

Three gates stand between us and a field, in this order: a second containment layer, a GMO release pathway, and a cost per device we have not yet totalled.

Open problems, as of 13 August 2026

What is not true yet

01

We have not measured our own transfer function. The Hill parameters we designed against are published values from a different organism at a different wavelength.

02

Cells do not grow inside the fibre lumen. Oxygen, feed, shear and temperature are all still on the table. We have candidates and no answer.

03

The second containment layer does not exist. The membrane retains cells; that is one net. We were told in July to build a second and we have not.

04

Delivery stops at a reservoir. The permeate pump is unspecified and the root-zone interface is deferred.

05

Latency is hours, not minutes. The optogenetic switch takes about 105 minutes to half-maximum, so this device is for creeping salinity, not for a shock.

06

Our photometer has one calibration point. No linearity curve, no limit of detection, no repeatability figure yet.
