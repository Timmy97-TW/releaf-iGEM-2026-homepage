What you were just looking at

A box of bacteria that answers to light

Salinity and temperature go in through a soil probe. An Arduino turns that reading into a PWM duty cycle on a 520 nm array. Inside a perfusion chamber, B. subtilis carrying the CcaSR optogenetic system transcribes ACC deaminase in proportion to that light. A 0.2 µm hollow-fibre membrane lets the enzyme out and keeps every cell in.

Nothing in the chain is a threshold. That is the engineering claim, and the reason the front of this page is a brightness rather than a diagram.

The machine

Three instruments, none of them bought

A perfusion bioreactor around a 0.2 µm hollow-fibre module. An in-line photometer that reads cell density without pulling a sample. An LED array that holds an intensity instead of blinking.

Part numbers, because anyone can write “peristaltic pump”: Biophsep™ mPES HF-E-MI-M020-10-60-P · 0.2 µm · 150 cm² · 8 fibres · 1.0 mm lumen. Arduino Uno R4, 14-bit ADC. IRLZ44N logic-level MOSFET. BP8G-AGA 0–1 MPa transducer.

The part that worked

Our photometer noticed the pump die

Stress → light has run since 20 June. Transmembrane pressure has been instrumented since 12 July. Then the in-line photometer logged for three straight weeks and caught a sharp terminal drop — the cross-flow pump failing. An instrument we built, finding a fault in the machine it was watching.

The sensing half of the loop is closed. The biology half is not, and we say so.

What would have to be true

One device, then one row

Scaling means replicating the 8-fibre module at constant wall shear and constant flux — the linear scale-up rule that carried a published process 400-fold without a pilot stage. Keep the physics at the membrane identical; let only the area grow.

Three gates stand between us and a field, in this order: a second containment layer, a GMO release pathway, and a cost per device we have not totalled.

Open problems · 13 August 2026

What is not true yet

01

We have not measured our own transfer function. The Hill parameters we designed against are published values, from a different organism at a different wavelength.

02

Cells do not grow inside the fibre lumen. Oxygen, feed, shear and temperature are all still candidates. We have no root cause.

03

The second containment layer does not exist. The membrane retains cells; that is one net. We were told in July to build a second and we have not.

04

Delivery stops at a reservoir. The permeate pump is unspecified and the root-zone interface is deferred.

05

Latency is hours, not minutes. About 105 minutes to half-maximum, so this is a device for creeping salinity, not for a shock.

06

Our photometer has one calibration point. No linearity curve, no limit of detection, no repeatability figure yet.
